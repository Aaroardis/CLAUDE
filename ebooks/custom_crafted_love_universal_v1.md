# Welcome…

Here's a question I love to ask the people I work with…

If you designed your love life from scratch - from your real values, your real desires, and a real understanding of what you and the people you love need in order to thrive - what would you build?

Take your time with that one. It's a big, beautiful question, and ours is one of the first generations truly free to ask it out loud.

When I share the world of ethical non-monogamy with my clients, I hear one sentence almost every week: "This would have saved my marriage." People aren't penguins… and the relationship model so many of us inherited was designed for social, political, and economic safety, long before anyone thought to design it around human happiness. You get to design yours around happiness - yours, your partner's, and everyone your love touches.

That's what custom-crafted love is. It might look like a marriage that stays wide open to adventure, a partnership with a little room for play, a whole constellation of loves, or a joyful monogamy you choose with your eyes wide open. Every one of them is beautiful when it's chosen on purpose.

> This is revolutionary relational work… it's new work, and it hasn't been done quite this way before, so be gracious and patient with yourself.

## The Art of Emotional Leadership

Here's what years of guiding people through open love have shown me: it's the most demanding personal development path I know… and one of the most free.

Inside a traditional structure, it's easy to manage around the parts of ourselves we haven't fully met yet - the feelings held a little too tightly, the needs never named out loud. Open love brings all of it to the surface, and fast. That's why it's such a powerful initiation, into what I call the art of emotional leadership: leading yourself first, with honesty and steadiness, and then leading your relationships with transparency, generosity, and care.

And you already know how to lead. You've built things - a career, a family, a home, a body of work - with vision, clear agreements, full disclosure, and calm under pressure. What the world forgot to give us was an education in leading from the heart, in the most intimate territory of our lives. That's exactly where the return lives. You master whatever you set your mind to, right? So focus on this!

Love has strategy, and love is also a living mystery that no strategy can fully hold. So everything in these pages is designed to make you more skillful AND more open to being surprised.

➢ Open relationship - a committed partnership with room for sexual connection outside it
➢ Ethical non-monogamy (ENM) - the umbrella term for any relationship in which everyone knowingly agrees to non-exclusivity

# PART ONE
# Be Shameless… With Yourself

Your private why, told to yourself first.

Before a single wall goes up, a great architect walks the land and learns its truth - the slope of the ground, the soil, the water running underneath. Your love life deserves that same honest survey… and here, the land is your own inner world.

So before you open anything, get radically honest with yourself about your why. I call it your private why: the real, unedited reason open love is calling you right now, told to yourself first, in total honesty, before you ever share it with anyone else.

> Total self-acceptance is the foundation UPON which "The Work" is done.

✳

## Say It All… to Yourself

At least to yourself, say all the "wrong" things, the taboo things, the things others might judge! We're on a rock flying through outer space - why not feel ALL of this crazy human experience?

So many of us feel pressure, collectively and individually, to hide our natural lust, our primal responses, our authentic desires and judgments, and every trait someone else has declared "unevolved." And you deserve a foundation of total self-acceptance, because you can only ascend from a self you fully OWN.

So ask yourself, and answer with total candor…

Are you feeling lust, ego, a desire for status, shame, confusion, hunger, a little cockiness?

Are you attracted to youth, beauty, variety, the idea of hot dates, having orgies, or being seen as sexy, cool, successful, or powerful?

Great! Let it in, let it be, love it… and pause there, in total honesty. From that pause, you can begin to dig deeper.

✳

## Every Why Is Workable

People come to open love for so many reasons, and every one of them is workable once you can name it out loud. Maybe…

➢ Your desire naturally leans toward a number of beautiful humans, and you'd love to make room for that truth
➢ You're curious about novelty, kink, or power, or about a bisexual or queer part of you that's been waiting for some air
➢ You've realized that the person who meets you most deeply in erotic expression may be a different person from the one who shares your home, your schedule, and your plans for the future
➢ Your partner is the one who wants to explore, and you're ready to celebrate that freedom
➢ You want every desire you have to live in the open, with nothing hidden
➢ You long for community - a village of people who show up for each other
➢ You love clear structure and explicit agreements, and the honesty they bring
➢ You want long-term stability with a co-parent, plus romance and adventure beyond the partnership

When you open from honesty, you carry honesty into every new connection you make… and you become exactly the kind of lover people trust with their freedom.

✳

## Main Course… or Dessert?

For some people, open love is the Main Course - the foundation of the self, and therefore of every relationship they build. It's philosophically meaningful to them, aligned with their deepest values, emotionally rich, and core to their sexual expression. For others it's Dessert: a delicious addition that comes into the foreground now and then, the cherry on top of a serious partnership - innocent fun, or a naughty night out.

Both are beautiful, so which one sounds like you right now?

✳

## Then Dig a Little Deeper

Once you've let everything in, take out a journal, find a quiet hour, and write your way into these…

➢ What am I really here for - a night, a season, a love, a life?
➢ Can I feel safe letting go of my positivity for a moment, to see what's really here?
➢ What do I want to build my future around, whatever my relationships look like?
➢ What would make me look back at the end of my life and say, "I'm so glad I devoted so much of my love and energy to that"?
➢ When I share my why with my partner, what do I hope they'll feel?

Your private why is the ground everything else will stand on… and once you know it, every conversation ahead gets clearer, calmer, and far more honest.

# PART TWO
# Soft, Steady, Secure

Becoming the nest - for yourself, and for the people you love.

Our lovers sense everything in the relational field, even what they can't yet put into words. We're all energetic perceivers, really - some of us are full-on emotional Jedi masters - and every heart is always seeking a field in which it can safely unfold. And here's the beautiful part: your partner unfolds to you as fully as you've unfolded to yourself.

When your emotions are fully felt and safe to YOU, a solid nest forms inside you - a place where your partner can come to rest, reveal themselves fully, and open body, mind, and heart. In open love, that nest matters more than ever, because new people and new dynamics bring every feeling to the surface. And from a foundation of softness and security, all things can be built.

✳

## Let Yourself Be Moved

When a big feeling arrives - longing, tenderness, a little fear, a rush of joy - notice what your body does. You might feel your chest tighten, your face go still, your throat clamp a little… and that's so natural, after a lifetime of learning to contain and bypass big emotions.

So take a beat, take a breath, and let the feeling move through you. You may find something rich and real and beautiful inside - some insight, some grief, some wonder. Then name it with precision, the way you'd name anything you care deeply about: "I feel elated, and also a little unsettled." Specific emotional language is a skill, and every time you use it, your nest grows stronger.

Underneath every feeling lives your Higher Self - a love inside you that can't be destabilized, because it IS your truest, deepest nature. That's your greatest strength… and I want you connected to it, always.

✳

## Jealousy Is a Signal

Jealousy will probably visit you at some point, because you're a human being who loves someone. When it does, welcome it as a signal pointing toward something tender that's asking for your care - a longing to be chosen, a need you haven't named yet, or an agreement that's ready to be revisited.

Get curious as fast as you can. Feel it all the way through, name it out loud, and let it become information… and a beautiful opportunity to be cared for.

Shaking a little and coming home to calm is mastery too, and it gets faster with practice. Here's how it goes…

➢ You hear something, and it lands a little hard somewhere in your body
➢ You notice the shake - heat in your chest, tightness in your throat, the urge to say something sharp
➢ You stay in the room, and you keep breathing
➢ You let it move through you, as pure information
➢ You come back to steady… then curious… then open, and maybe even eager to hear more

And try turning the old story, the way I do with my clients…

"My partner is out with someone new tonight, and I'm home alone."
⇣
My partner is out filling their cup, and that light is coming home to us.
Tonight is mine to enjoy, too.

"Everything is on my shoulders."
⇣
I'm learning the art of emotional leadership - of my relationship, and of myself.
This chapter is an initiation, and what I'm learning now will carry me.

✳

## A Lot of Possibility… A Little Fear

Here's a fear that surprises so many of my clients: the fear that things could go really, really well. A new love could bring immense emotional and spiritual growth, shift the fabric of your family, and change your whole life… and that is SUBLIME. I love that word, because it describes something so beautiful that it brings overwhelm and perhaps a hint of terror.

What do you do with that feeling? Drink it in! Be MOVED by the possibility that life could surprise you completely. That's what life is FOR.

> Is love risky? YES! Is love safe? YES! Love is… everything.

✳

## Soft, Steady, Secure… Together

Before you talk about who, or rules, or apps, have the conversation that makes everything else graceful: what does each of you need in order to feel secure?

Then create a simple ritual of affirmations that feel soft and true - true meaning they live in the authentic overlap of your needs. Read these together, and choose the ones that feel true for you both…

➢ Today I choose you.
➢ I love you more and more.
➢ I'm here. I want to be here.
➢ We're in a process of learning.
➢ I trust our process of discovery.
➢ We're a good match.
➢ I trust myself to love you well, and I trust you to love me well.
➢ I will try to love you as you need to be loved.
➢ Our relationship is important.

Each of you, add one of your own, write them all down, and keep them where you'll see them. Then speak them aloud to each other - perhaps every night after dinner, with a candle lit, holding hands, with five minutes of eye gazing. A ritualized return to shared values brings deep peace… and when you return to your affirmations of secure love, over and over, more becomes possible.

# PART THREE
# Informed Consent Requires… You Informing

Transparency is the most magnetic move there is.

Picture a table where every card is face up - your desires, your limits, what you can offer, what you're hoping for. There's a particular calm that settles over two people when they can both see the whole hand… and that calm is the beginning of trust.

In open love, a lover can only make an informed decision about how they'd love to engage with you when you inform them. Your transparency is very sexy. It's your opportunity to lead your life with integrity. And it's your lover's opportunity to be in integrity with their own wants and needs. There's a conscious, grounded move here, and you already KNOW what it is… put your cards on the table. You can be SUAVE AF while you're being totally transparent.

> There's nothing sexier than someone who knows what they want… and what they want to GIVE.

✳

## Opening the Conversation With Your Partner

If you're the one who'd love to open your relationship, the first person you inform is your partner - and the way you bring it to them matters enormously. It takes courage, because when you're fully honest, you might hear a no, and you care so much about their heart. Share it anyway, generously and in advance of anything happening.

➢ Choose a calm, unhurried moment - an evening with nowhere to be, far from any conflict
➢ Lead with your love for each other and for everything you share, then offer your private why in plain, tender words
➢ Welcome every response - curiosity, excitement, tears, a little fear - because every feeling in the room is good information
➢ Give it real time, days or even weeks, so you can both feel into it before anything is decided
➢ Explore it together, Goldilocks-style: "What would feel like too much for you? What would feel like not enough? What would feel just right?"
➢ Then come back to the security conversation from Part Two, before any talk of who or how

You might begin something like this… "I love you, and I love what we've built together. Something's been stirring in me, and I'd love to explore it with you - with total honesty, and with all the time you need. Can we get curious about it together?"

(This is one of my very favorite conversations to hold for couples - the timing, the first words, and receiving each other's real reactions with warmth.)

And if your partner is the one who brings it to you, you've just been handed a gift: their honesty. Receive it with the same warmth you'd hope for… Part Four is all about exactly that.

✳

## Put Your Cards on the Table

Here's how it can sound, with a new lover…

"I'd love to kiss you, touch you, and fulfill your wild fantasies this weekend. I'm free for flirty messages this week, to keep your body at an erotic hum. I'm in an open relationship with a partner I adore, and that partnership comes first in my life. Tell me your desires and your limits, and let's see if there's a real overlap. I engage with clear boundaries - and I'd love for you to, as well."

Then give your new lover room to decide… "Please feel into that. When you're ready, I'd love to hear what you want." It takes guts to be this clear. (And if you don't know yet? Say so, and promise nothing until you do.)

A true meeting of minds needs clarity - your authentic needs and your lover's authentic boundaries, both on the table. That clarity keeps a light connection light and lovely, and it lets a deep one grow on solid ground.

✳

## Poly 101… and Poly 202

Here's a simple way to hold the two levels of honesty in open love.

➢ Poly 101 is transparency - telling your partners who else you're seeing, what attachment you have to them, and sharing your sexual health information
➢ Poly 202 is consent - agreeing together BEFORE you shift the relational container by adding a new partner, and supporting each other's emotional responses to that shift

Poly 101 keeps everyone informed. Poly 202 keeps everyone choosing, together.

✳

## Tell It Early, Tell It Warmly

➢ Tell it early. The truth told beforehand is a plan… and that same truth told afterward is a confession. Same words, entirely different relationship.
➢ Ask how much detail your partner wants. Some people love the whole picture, warmly and specifically, and others want the headline. Both are completely legitimate, and the answer changes over time, so ask again.
➢ Share for connection. Share what your partner needs in order to consent, participate, and stay close to you - and when you feel the urge to narrate every passing attraction, pause and ask yourself whether you're building connection or looking for reassurance.
➢ Welcome the whole response. Try: "I want to tell you something, and I want you to be able to have whatever response you have." Then make all the room your partner needs, without rushing anyone back toward fine.
➢ Repair quickly. When you notice you've been holding something, simply name it: "I've been holding this, and I want to tell you why."

✳

## Before Anything Opens: Sexual Health and the Questions That Matter

Agree on sexual health together - testing history, testing rhythm, barriers, and what you'll use with whom - BEFORE any sexual touch happens outside your bond, and never in the heat of the moment. It's one of the most beautiful trust-builders there is.

And with every new lover, start the conversations that make someone feel deeply safe with you…

➢ Is sex a bonding experience for you?
➢ Do you want our connection to change after sex?
➢ What's your testing rhythm, and what protection feels good to us both?
➢ If pregnancy is possible between us, how would you like to handle prevention?
➢ What will you want or need from me in the hour after sex… and in the week after?

Be grounded, be conscious, and create the container… so your lover can relax into DESIRE for you.

# PART FOUR
# Free of Limits

When your partner opens too… or first.

Picture a flower filling out its space - unfurling in its own time, toward its own light, until it's fully and gloriously itself. That's your partner's freedom, blooming beside a lover who's secure enough to celebrate it.

There's a Ram Dass teaching I love to pass along, with my own spin: your mission as a partner and lover is to be an environment in which your beloved can be free of every limiting belief they hold about themselves… and free of any limiting belief YOU might hold about their potential. So tell your beloved, often… "You can become any evolved version of yourself. All love and all possibility are yours. You're limitless."

> For long-term happiness, every one of us needs some short-term fun.

✳

## Why Their Desire Matters

Every one of us is sovereign, and in this moment so many people are claiming a freedom and a pleasure that are brand new in human history. So when your partner shares a desire - to explore a new side of their sexuality, to take a lover, to go dancing and play - something powerful happens when the person they love legitimizes that desire, out loud.

So say it, in your own words…

"Your desire is beautiful to me. I want you to have joy, adventure, and pleasure that's all your own. You deserve to feel fully alive… and I'm proud to be beside you while you do."

And keep the spotlight right where it belongs - on your partner. (The reflex to steer their desire toward your own favorite fantasy is so human it's practically a rite of passage. Let it pass with a smile!) Their desire gets to be about THEM.

✳

## Asymmetry Is Part of the Plan

Your partner's opening may look nothing like yours, and that's often exactly right. They may explore while you hold space for a season. You may date while they're happily home with a book. Closeness, affection, and support from many directions were meant to be a far bigger part of every human life than our culture has made room for - so if your partner is craving closeness with others, celebrate it, and enjoy your freedom from the impossible job of being everything for one person.

Asymmetry is fine, and it's part of the plan.

✳

## The One Who Brought That Joy

Sexual energy is life force. When your partner comes home lit up, that light pours back into their whole life - work, creativity, body, friendships… and you. And you become the one who brought them that joy.

That warm, generous feeling has a name: compersion, the pleasure you feel at your partner's pleasure with someone else. It's real, and it's available. For some people it arrives right away, and for others it sneaks up sweetly after years of trust… and your love can flourish either way.

✳

## Your Unspoken Fear

Let's gently name the fear so many of us carry quietly: what if they don't come back?

It's such a tender, human fear, and underneath it lives something even more tender - a longing to be chosen, again and again. So honor it - say it out loud to yourself, to a trusted friend, and, when you're ready, to your partner. Ask for the reassurance you need, and build rituals that make being chosen beautifully visible: a quick text on arrival, another on the way home, and the particular way your partner walks back through the door.

# PART FIVE
# Ask for Love With Pride

Dating in open love.

New love is a new road. It's smooth and thrilling, and it's easy to hit cruise control and simply enjoy the view… and the wisest drivers keep their hands gently on the wheel, eyes on the road, present to the curves and the possibilities ahead.

So ask for love with pride, and watch who steps forward, saying, "Yes, you're marvelous and unique and adorable, and I'd love to learn how to love you!" You'll see what's real… and you can build from there.

> The project of life is LOVE.

✳

## Align… and Let Them Choose

The most magnetic people I work with get to know new lovers gradually, extend themselves incrementally, and build relational containers that feel safe and real. They look for authentic alignment and let competing for love fall away. They make room for every lover to be an autonomous decision maker - someone who names their own needs and chooses their own course - and they stay beautifully secure through every choice.

And you can do exactly the same. Release the old habit of strategizing to get someone to do what you want, show up with your real emotions, real desires, and real self… and let a lover step forward to meet the real you, or step back and find better alignment. Either way, everyone wins.

✳

## Be Real, Beautifully

If you're a high performer, you're probably asked to describe yourself as a heart-forward, visionary leader all the time - and it's seductive to spin a grand vision of who you're becoming. The more magnetic move is radical realness: speak openly about where you've done your work and where you're still evolving. To anyone grounded in their own knowing, that honesty resonates… and it's a real foundation for love.

And here's something so hopeful. People dream about partners who smile warmly, listen earnestly, and make dinner - partners they can trust to learn, adapt, get resourceful, keep their chin up, and make things work. Every one of those is a quality you can cultivate.

✳

## Your Bio, Your Truth

Really owning who you are and what you love is the BEST way to find a good match - and in open love, your transparency begins before the first date. Say it right in your profile…

➢ "I'm in an open relationship with a partner I adore, and we're both free to date."
➢ "I'm looking for [playful adventures / a lasting connection / something we'll discover together]."
➢ "I love direct, communicative people - please tell me everything!"
➢ "Honesty and kindness matter enormously to me."

Then, before you drive across town to meet a stranger, try a ten- or twenty-minute video chat… "I'd love to have a quick chat on Zoom or FaceTime so we can get to know each other a little. Are you up for a quick online vibe check?"

✳

## Say What You Want… and Say What You Don't

Let's destigmatize casual sex. You'd love sex a la carte? Great! A season of adventure? Great! A deep, ongoing love? Beautiful! Every one of these can be healthy and happy when there's transparency - and it's far kinder to say "Right now I'm here for pleasure and connection" than to promise a relationship because it sounds more evolved. You're free to say "I don't know yet," too… and to promise nothing until you do.

And when it comes to time, remember that love is limitless… and time is not. So check in with yourself before each new connection…

➢ Do I have the time to get to know someone new, while I keep showing my partner the affection they deserve?
➢ Do I have the emotional energy for curiosity, for challenge, and for repair?
➢ Can I offer every person involved a rich experience?

✳

## Bonding Chemistry, Tender Care

One more thing to hold with great care. After sex, many people feel a powerful surge of bonding chemistry. Oxytocin - the bonding hormone - rises with intimate touch, and for some of us its pull is especially strong, sometimes long before anyone has decided what the connection is. So talk about it before, honor it after, and ask the question that makes you an extraordinary lover in open love…

"How can I take beautiful care of your heart?"

✳

## Your Field Guide for New Love

➢ Go slowly with your new love
➢ Gather knowledge of each other through accumulated experiences together
➢ Ask real questions, seek real answers, and show your real self
➢ Discuss red flags and yellow flags with clear eyes and level heads
➢ Explain what you need, ask what your lover needs, and do your best to meet those needs
➢ Find the overlap of what you both truly want and truly enjoy… and focus your energy there
➢ Be affectionate - laugh, dance, play, romp in nature, cook, make art, make love, have FUN!
➢ Listen well, and apologize sincerely when it's right

# PART SIX
# Coming Home

The hour after, and the days after.

Every home has a threshold - the doorway where the world outside meets the world within. In open love, some of your most important moments happen right there, when your partner steps back across it after a night with someone new… or when you do.

The night itself is often the easy part. The next three days are where love does its most tender, important work, so tend them on purpose.

> Couples who thrive in open love speak - their hopes AND their fears, out loud.

✳

## Make the Pregame a Date

Before your partner heads out, make the evening a date of its own. Put on their favorite music for getting ready, let them choose exactly what to wear, and bring pure enthusiasm to every option - your partner deserves to feel DIVINE walking out that door. Then agree on a simple check-in: a text on arrival, and a text on the way home. In between, trust that hours of silence usually mean a wonderful night is unfolding.

✳

## Your Night In

While your partner is out, let your evening be a gift to yourself. See a friend, cook something wonderful, move your body, make something with your hands, or simply rest. Pour your energy into work you love, friends you love, a healthy mind, and a healthy body… because a happy relationship is most likely to emerge from a happy YOU.

✳

## The Homecoming Menu

Offer your partner a homecoming menu in advance, to choose from the moment they walk back through the door…

➢ Pancakes at 3am
➢ "Tell me everything!"
➢ Being softly held
➢ A warm bath
➢ Quiet time alone to integrate

Then let your partner lead the first touch. A kiss they reach for feels like a welcome, and a kiss you rush toward can feel like reclaiming territory. If they'd love to sleep in a separate bed that night, welcome it as a reset - a gentle landing - and trust the closeness that follows.

✳

## When You're the One Coming Home

Bring that same generosity home with you: a warm arrival, full presence, and exactly the level of detail your partner has asked for, offered with tenderness. And try this before you walk in. Stop at the door - hand on the handle, feet on the ground - and take three slow breaths. Say to yourself, "I am grounded. I am powerful. I am loving." and MEAN it. Let your body truly ARRIVE in grounded love before you meet your partner's eyes… and they'll FEEL the difference before you've said a single word.

✳

## Cool Downs and Check-Ins

When things feel intense - or simply for the sweetness of it - ask each other for a Cool Down: hold hands, sway to a slow song, and let go of expectations for a moment.

And during any big stretch of new experiences, run down this list together once a day. Right now, are you getting enough…

➢ Sleep, food, water, and body comfort?
➢ Down time, restful time, or time alone?
➢ Play, adventure, and fun?
➢ Collaborative decision making… and independent decision making?
➢ Appreciation of each other's presence and effort?
➢ Emotional witnessing and support?

✳

## Hopes and Fears, Out Loud

Every so often, give your connection a beautiful container - an hour on the couch with candles lit and something sweet to share. This is a space for softness, truth, and deep listening. Take turns with questions like…

➢ Is there a boundary you've recognized inside yourself that you haven't shared yet?
➢ Is there anywhere in our connection you feel you're over-giving?
➢ What's too much evolution for us right now… and what's too little?
➢ How do you need to be loved this week? This month?

✳

## Repair, Softly

When something tender happens - a hurt feeling, a boundary crossed by accident - meet it with immense graciousness. Gently ask what happened, listen with compassion while keeping the focus on your partner's experience, and apologize sincerely when it's right. Then let the repair be soft: tea, slow music, a walk, a nap, stargazing. PEACE IS A CHOICE YOU CAN MAKE IN EVERY MOMENT… and you get to make it together.

# PART SEVEN
# Branches That Bend Don't Break

Agreements that grow with you.

Picture a great old tree in a windstorm. Its roots hold deep, its branches bend and sway with every gust… and when the storm passes, it's still standing, taller for everything it weathered. Its flexibility IS its strength.

> Allow love to grow you in the most surprising ways.

✳

## Vision Before Rules

So many open relationships begin with a list of restrictions. Start somewhere warmer: with what you're building toward. Ask each other…

➢ What do we want MORE of in our life together?
➢ What does each of us want to EXPLORE, and what draws us there?
➢ How do we want to FEEL at the end of a night when one of us has been out?
➢ What does a glorious future version of us look like, three years from now?

Agreements come afterward, as structures that serve the vision.

✳

## Name Your Hierarchy Out Loud

If your partner has priority - weekends, holidays, the final word when two plans collide - say so, warmly and clearly, to everyone involved, including every new lover. People can happily consent to a clear structure… and a clear structure is a gift to everyone who lives inside it.

✳

## Who Else Gets to Know?

Decide together how open you'll be beyond your relationship - with friends, family, children, colleagues, and the communities you belong to. Will you share broad strokes, or details? What will your lovers know about your partner? Will your children meet the people you come to love? Whatever you decide is beautiful… just decide it together, and revisit it as your life changes.

✳

## Keep Your Agreements Alive

An agreement made in month one, by two people who've never done this before, is a beautiful first draft. Write it down, put a review date on it, and notice which agreements are load-bearing and which you'd happily renegotiate. When you need the relationship to shift, adjust the agreements explicitly, out loud, together… because branches that bend don't break.

✳

## Growth and Stability

Some relationships are built for stability - consistency, safety, and the reassurance of routines. Others are built for growth - exploratory, exciting, challenging, deepening. Both are beautiful, and both are perfect. So ask each other, now and then…

What project of relationship are we in right now?

Do we want more stability, or more growth?

And if we want both, how do we create the balance?

And DO get attached - to each other, to your agreements, and above all to the process of evolving together, welcoming every change that comes.

✳

## Conscious Coupling

Here's a vision I love: modern rituals that celebrate conscious coupling - intentions to be together for as long as there's love and learning. Imagine promising one year of intentional love, looking deeply at everything you've learned together… and then choosing one more year, wide awake and wholehearted. A relationship chosen again and again stays wonderfully alive.

✳

## Release With Gratitude

Sometimes a romance completes its purpose. Release it as gently as you can, with great gratitude… and remember that you can keep a lasting bond, if it's still precious to you both. Relationships can shape your heart in the most beautiful ways without lasting forever, and growth is healthy, and always worth celebrating.

## Your People

It's so common to walk this path without telling a single friend, carrying something enormous all on your own. And strength looks like reaching out. Tell a trusted friend what you're exploring, and try saying, "Hey… I need to work through a feeling here, and I need to be messier than usual. Can you just listen for five or ten minutes? I just need the witnessing." We can't be fully accepted unless we're fully seen… so let your people see you.

Then turn it around, and ask a friend the three questions I love most…

➢ How's your heart today?
➢ What's the biggest hurt you're holding right now?
➢ And what evolution are we celebrating - what can your heart do now that it couldn't do last year?

We all need a village - close friendships, nurturing community, and an epic support team of coaches, therapists, or practitioners who inspire you to rise.
