## HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

* Every TEDx event is independently licensed and organized. TED staff do not identify or place speakers. Applications go event by event.
* Talks are capped at 18 minutes.
* TED rules prohibit selling products, promoting a business, or self-promotion from the stage. The pitch is a claim about society; the coaching practice is evidence base only, and appears in the bio.
* TEDx lineups are required to be multidisciplinary — organizers must fill a range of subject areas, and the culture / relationships slot is a distinct category from the business, health, and technology applicants who dominate most pools.
* Most events open applications 6–9 months ahead of the event date and publish the window only when it opens.

## ADJACENT OPPORTUNITIES

**TED Fellows** — Nomination-only since 2024. No public application. Nominators: 500+ current Fellows plus program friends. Selection criteria: emerging leaders not yet on the global stage; originality and on-the-ground work; collaborative character; positioned to use amplification, network-building, and communication training.

**TED2027** — TED's flagship conference, San Diego, March 1–4, 2027. First California flagship since the conference's origins. Attendance places Aaro among Fellows and TED curators.

**TED speaker nomination** — ted.com/participate/nominate. Self-nomination accepted. Goes directly to TED's speaker selection team.

**SXSW PanelPicker** — Separate organization from TED. Community-voted proposals. 2027 window closed July 26, 2026. Next window: June 2027 for SXSW 2028 (Austin, March). Formats include Presentation, Panel, Workshop, Core Conversation, Fireside Chat, Book Reading.
