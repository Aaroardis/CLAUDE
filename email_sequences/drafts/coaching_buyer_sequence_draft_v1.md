Preview: Contemporary attachment theory, made practical - plus the beautiful news about earned security.

Hey Love…

Here's something that thrills me about the science of love: the way you bond keeps growing for your entire life.

Contemporary attachment theory is one of the frameworks I'm trained in and use with my clients. It maps the patterns your nervous system learned about closeness - and every pattern is intelligent. Each one was a brilliant strategy for staying connected… and each one can evolve.

[PICTURE]

Here's a quick, loving tour of the four styles…

* Secure - closeness feels natural, space feels comfortable, and you ask for what you need with ease
* Anxious - you feel love deeply and crave reassurance, especially when connection feels uncertain
* Avoidant - you treasure independence, and a lot of closeness can make you crave a little distance and space
* Fearful-avoidant - you long for closeness and feel a pull away from it at the same time

We all carry a blend, and it can shift with different partners and different seasons of life.

And here's the most exciting part… Researchers call it "earned security" - the secure attachment you build as an adult, through self-awareness, practice, and relationships that feel safe. Watching it bloom in a client is one of my favorite things in the world.

Try this: remember the last time you felt a little unsure where you stood with someone you love. What did your body want to do - move closer, pull back, or both?
