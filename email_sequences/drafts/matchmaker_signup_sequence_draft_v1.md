## matchmaker-03 | Your first-date practice
Timing: 1 week later
Subject: Your first-date practice (try it on your very next date)
Alt subject: Three questions that make a first date unforgettable
Preview: A simple, powerful way to turn small talk into real connection.

Hey, Hot Stuff…

Your next date is a beautiful opportunity - and today I'm giving you a practice to make it unforgettable.

[PICTURE]

✨ THE JUICY PART - today, it gets the whole email!

Ask Real Questions. Seek Real Answers. Show Your Real Self. On your next date, bring three questions that go a layer deeper than small talk, and let the conversation unfold from there. Try…

"What's something you're excited about right now?"
"What does a really good day look like for you?"
"What are you hoping for in this chapter of your life?"

Then listen all the way to the end of every answer. Let a pause breathe before you respond. And when it's your turn, answer with the same openness - one true, specific thing about you.

Go slowly, and gather knowledge of each other from real experiences together. That's how a first date becomes the beginning of something extraordinary.
