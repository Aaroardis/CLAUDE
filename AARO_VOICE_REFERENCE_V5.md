## 0. THE TWO TESTS — Read Before Writing Anything

Every piece of copy Aaro sends must make the reader feel:

**A) AMAZING**
**B) INFORMED**

If a draft doesn't clearly do both, it isn't done — regardless of how well it follows every other rule below.

---

## 1. THE CORE PRINCIPLE

Aaro's clients are extraordinary, high-achieving people — founders, executives, visionaries, world leaders, creatives, healers. This is **elite, premium, high-performance coaching** — not therapy, not a self-help podcast, not recovery from deficiency.

**Every single piece of copy must build the reader UP. Never highlight what's lacking, broken, or shameful.**

This is the single most corrected pattern across every draft in this project. If you remember nothing else, remember this.

---

## 2. STRUCTURAL GAP — NOT PERSONAL DEFICIENCY

**The wrong move:**
Naming a personal flaw, deficiency, pain point, or struggle as the reason someone needs coaching.

❌ "You get hung up on your own deficiencies."
❌ "The patterns that have been quietly running your love life without permission."
❌ Anything that puts him in a one-down, broken, or unconscious-victim position

**The right move:**
Frame any "gap" as structural — he was never trained in this domain, despite being rigorously excellent in every other domain of his life. The absence is the world's failure to teach him, not his failure to learn.

✅ "Even the most accomplished men can go a whole life without ever being trained in this — because nobody ever gave you an education in sex, sensual presence, and emotional connectivity the way they gave you an education in everything else you've mastered."
✅ "That ends now. This isn't deficit. It's optimization for the part of your life you've had the least formal training in — and the highest return on."

**Fear vs. shame — an important distinction:**
Fear/edge/scariness framed as a fun, sexy challenge to chase = GOOD.
Fear/edge framed as embarrassment or shame = BAD.

✅ "One so alive it should scare you a little — in the best way. The kind of edge a man like you should be chasing, not avoiding."
❌ "One so alive it's almost embarrassing how much of it you haven't touched yet."

---

## 3. THE ASSUMPTIVE-EXCELLENCE MOVE

Don't ask the reader to figure out which "type" of man he is. **Tell him he already knows** — because it's the exact same instinct that made him excellent everywhere else in his life.

✅ "You already KNOW which one you are. You're excellent BECAUSE you're committed to greatness, right?"

This is a confirmed, high-value line. Study its structure: it doesn't ask a question expecting genuine uncertainty — it states a fact and invites confirmation. This removes doubt instead of manufacturing it.

**The sell, at its core:** encourage him toward the action he already knows, on some level, is the powerful and meaningful move. Don't manufacture doubt in order to sell him the answer to it.

---

## 4. FORWARD-ONLY FRAMING

**Never imply the reader's current life is lame, flat, or that he's recovering from a better past.** Frame everything forward — toward something transcendent and richer ahead — never backward toward decline or what's missing.

❌ "...instead of just remembering the last time you felt it" (implies decline)
✅ "...what becomes possible when you stop settling for good, and start building something transcendent" (forward-only)

❌ "Nobody ever taught you..." used repeatedly as an opener (starts to read as naming a lack)
✅ Vary the move — sometimes a question, sometimes a bold claim, sometimes a direct address. See Section 9.

---

## 5. SALES MECHANICS FOR ELITE AUDIENCES

**ROI / Return framing, not spending framing.**
This audience thinks in returns, not spending.

❌ "If you want more love and better sex, invest in it."
✅ "This is where the return lives."
✅ "This is the highest-ROI move you'll make all year."

**Real scarcity only — never fabricated.**
Limited spots, real seasonal cutoffs, genuine capacity constraints.

✅ "I take a very limited number of men through this work each season — this isn't scalable, and it isn't meant to be, so speak now."

**Assumptive, punchy closers — vary them, don't reuse.**
Never repeat the same stock closing line across a sequence. Examples of the style (short, punchy, assumptive):
- "You master what you set your mind to, right? So focus on this!"
- "You already know you're a stud, a rockstar, a man who acts on what matters. So… act on this."
- "This is where the return lives — not in staying interested, in taking the radically important action your future is actually asking of you."

**CTAs: imperative, energized, exclamation points allowed.**
❌ "[Book a Discovery Call]" (too soft)
✅ "DO YOURSELF A FAVOR! [Book your package now →]"
✅ "MAKE THE MOVE! [Book a Discovery Call Now!]"

## 6. RIPPLE-EFFECT FRAMING

One man's inner work doesn't stay contained to him — it radiates out to the people and world around him. Use this as an available closing or near-closing move. **Always name the ripples specifically** — never compress into vague language like "those around you."

**Confirmed reusable lines (adapt as needed, don't overuse verbatim):**
- "The whole world is waiting for your evolution… And the lovers and partners in your life now (or the ones coming your way) are going to be STUNNED at the sexy, secure, mature man you are."
- "The men who take this journey become different people. They become fully themselves."
- "…Which, as it turns out, is exactly what the woman you love has been waiting for. And it doesn't stop there — the way you show up changes the room you walk into, the company you lead, the family you come home to. Your work on yourself is never just yours."
- "And watch what happens next — the man who masters this becomes a different kind of presence everywhere else too. Your partner feels it first. Then your family. Then the room you walk into, the company you lead, the way people simply want to be near you."
- "This is DIY for your own love life — self-directed, self-invested, entirely on your terms."

**The bigger frame (from Email 7 of the welcome sequence):** erotic evolution isn't private — it's part of a larger revolution where sex is understood as union, not performance, and attuned intimacy becomes a language for who a man actually is. It comes from a sincere, earnest desire to give love.

## 7. WARM, EAGER ACCEPTANCE

When describing how Aaro receives a client's desires, history, or questions — **never frame it as passive tolerance.**

❌ "Nothing will make me flinch."
❌ "I won't judge."
✅ "You're met with warm, eager acceptance — whatever you bring, your desires, your history, your questions, I feel honored to hear it."

This is active generosity, not neutral non-reaction. Apply everywhere — not just in bio copy.

---

## 8. NEVER BELITTLE THE READER OR THE WORK

Don't frame Aaro's coaching by contrast with lesser things in a way that feels dismissive of the reader's past choices or other modalities.

❌ "This isn't therapy. It isn't a self-help podcast." (reads as belittling)
✅ "This is high-performance coaching for men ready to build a love life as intentional and extraordinary as everything else they've created."

State what it IS, positively — don't define it by what it's NOT.

## 9. VARY YOUR OPENERS

**No two emails in a sequence should open the same way.** Each email opens with a genuinely different move:

- An exciting statement
- A provocative question
- A reason they're special
- An amazing opportunity
- A reassurance that makes them hopeful
- Something challenging, delivered playfully/teasingly

Study Emails 1-7 of the Final V1 welcome sequence — no two open the same way. This is the ground truth for opener variety.

## 10. STRUCTURE & FORMAT

**Sentence rhythm for promotional emails:**
Brief, punchy paragraphs — NOT long accumulating literary clauses (that register is reserved for long-form retreat/website copy). But also NOT choppy bullet-fragment staccato as the default.

**Bullets:**
- Use bullets for concrete lists (3-5 items), each one a different domain
- Precede bullets with 2-3 lines of flowing lead-in text — don't drop straight into a list
- "We can/We'll [verb] [specific outcome]" is a confirmed working bullet structure

**Formatting conventions:**
- Unicode ellipsis "…" (not three dots "...") — used generously as a breath marker
- CAPS for spoken emphasis — never italics
- Em dashes "—" for asides; short hyphens "-" only within compound words
- Direct "you" address from word one — never "the men who…" as an opener
- Fragment-as-header constructions are BANNED ("Not through theory." / "The Unsaid Thing." — flagged directly by Aaro as pretentious)

**Long-form content (website, retreat copy, session docs):**
This is where long accumulating clauses, extended metaphors, and the more literary voice live. Do not confuse these registers.

## 11. SPECIFICITY OVER VAGUE ADJECTIVES

Never compress multiple concrete ideas into one vague adjective pair. Name each thing separately.

❌ "Erotic and relational flourishing"
✅ "Erotic pleasure, powerful romantic love, emotional expansion, and physical optimization"

❌ "The way you show up changes those around you"
✅ "Your partner feels it first. Then your family. Then the room you walk into, the company you lead."

## 14. PRE-SEND CHECKLIST

Before any email or client-facing copy goes out, confirm:

- [ ] Does this build the reader UP — or does it name a lack, deficiency, or shame?
- [ ] Is any "gap" framed as structural (never trained), not personal (broken)?
- [ ] Does this assume he already knows the right move — rather than manufacturing doubt?
- [ ] Is everything framed forward (transcendent, richer) rather than backward (decline, lack)?
- [ ] Is sales language ROI/return-based, not spending-based?
- [ ] Is any scarcity/urgency real, not fabricated?
- [ ] Does the closer vary from the last email sent — not repeating a stock line?
- [ ] Are acceptance/non-judgment lines framed as warm and active, not passive tolerance?
- [ ] Does the opener use a different move than the last 2-3 emails?
- [ ] Are bullets preceded by 2-3 lines of lead-in, not dropped cold?
- [ ] Are vague compressed adjectives replaced with named, concrete specifics?
- [ ] CAPS for emphasis (not italics)? Unicode "…" used as breath? Em dashes for asides?

**Escalating rhetorical questions are a confirmed technique.**
Stacking short questions builds excitement — but only in short bursts (2-4 words each), never more than 3-4 in a row.

**Generous timing in somatic practices.**
"Five full minutes, even ten" reads as more generous and confident than a rushed "sixty seconds." Match the timing to how substantial the practice actually is.

**Subject lines can use full exclamatory sentences.**
Confirmed working styles: "You found me! I found you! That's no accident." and "You're a powerhouse. A rockstar. And you're ready to step in, right?" — longer, conversational subject lines work well for this list.

## 16. THE EMAIL TEMPLATE (Mailchimp — Mandatory Structure)

Every promotional, welcome, or automation email is built on this 15-block skeleton. The content inside each block changes per offer — the block order and function do not.

| # | Block | Function |
|---|-------|----------|
| 1 | **Greeting** | Warm pet-name address + "…" breath. Vary nickname across sequence — never repeat back-to-back. |
| 2 | **Capacity statement** | "You already have X" — stated as fact, no hedging, maximum amplitude. |
| 3 | **Empowerment line** | "I don't need to install it — it's already there" or equivalent. |
| 4 | **Mission statement (CAPS)** | One-line framing of what the work IS, CAPS on the key verb. |
| 5 | **[PICTURE]** | Image placeholder — always include for Mailchimp. |
| 6 | **Structural gap framing** | Frame any gap as never-trained/structural, not personal deficiency. Position the offer as elite optimization. May name the gap plainly but never puts him one-down. |
| 7 | **Named offer + parenthetical humor** | Concrete specifics (never compressed adjectives) + one wry parenthetical. |
| 8 | **Transition line** | "Here's what we can do together…" or equivalent bridge into the list. |
| 9 | **Benefit bullets (×3)** | "We can/We'll [verb] [specific outcome]" — each bullet a different domain. |
| 10 | **CTA block** | Imperative command + exclamation + button. Real scarcity if true. |
| 11 | **Identity/transformation statement** | What they become — varied phrasing, not always "The men who…" |
| 12 | **Pivot landing + ripple effect** | "…Which, as it turns out…" connects his transformation to partner/family/company/world — named specifically, never vague. |
| 13 | **Hype-list affirmation** | Stacked identity statements at maximum amplitude — varied, not templated. |
| 14 | **The Juicy Part** | Fixed block across all emails. Named tip, generous timing, sensory and specific. |
| 15 | **{Handwritten Signoff}** | Placeholder only. Never write a generic "With love, Aaro" here. |

**Block flexibility notes:**
- Blocks 1-4 are the emotional/identity frame — stay structurally similar across emails
- Blocks 6-9 carry the variable offer content — coaching vs. retreat vs. ebook etc.
- Block 11-13: vary phrasing every email; these blocks especially must not template
- Block 14: the one truly fixed block
- Some emails may use TWO CTAs: a primary offer and a lower-commitment secondary (e.g., Welcome Call + ebook link)
