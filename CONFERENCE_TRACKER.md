## Strategic note (read before booking travel)

Highest conversion-per-handshake is **not** the topic-matched ENM events (lower income, peer-saturated) — it's the **affluent-seeker rooms** (A-Fest, Wisdom 2.0, Conscious Entrepreneur, WOHASU) where Aaro is often the *only* intimacy coach present.

- **Topic events** (Beyond the Love, tantra) → build authority + content.
- **Affluent-seeker events** → book Welcome Calls.

Weight travel budget toward the latter. **One primary CTA at every event: book the Welcome Call.** Low-friction secondary: playbooks.
