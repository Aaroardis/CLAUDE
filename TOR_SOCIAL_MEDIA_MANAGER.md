## 11. Sales Conversion: Aaro's Two Streams

There are two streams for success in conversion, and both matter. They are measured differently.

**High-Volume — the primary stream on social media.** Ebooks, audios, live challenges, and the monthly subscription. Social media prioritizes this stream, because it generates revenue and broad impact at scale. These offers carry no calendar cost and no ceiling. Success is sales volume and reach into new audiences, measured on what they earn.

**High-Touch — nested within it.** 1:1 coaching, couples and polycule work, premium packages, and private retreats. A subset of the high-volume audience engages more and more deeply over time, moving toward the premium offerings. Success here is qualified conversations and booked clients — fit rather than volume.

Track which content produces clicks, DMs, purchases, and qualified leads, and use conversion data to refine future CTAs, offers, content topics, and sequencing.
